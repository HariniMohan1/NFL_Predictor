How Monte Carlo simulation works

Each of the 10,000 runs follows the same four steps

1 · Flip weighted coin
ELO 40% + EPA 40% + TO 20%
→
2 · Seed bracket
7-team playoff per conf.
→
3 · Simulate playoffs
WC → Div → Conf → SB
→
4 · Record winner
Repeat × 10,000
→
SB% = wins ÷ 10,000

Why XGBoost — not these alternatives
✕
Logistic Regression
Can't capture non-linear interactions. ELO × injury matters — logistic misses it entirely.
✕
Neural Networks
Needs 50K+ samples. We have 2,227 games. Would overfit and produce no SHAP output.
✕
Random Forest
Bagging not boosting — slower convergence. XGBoost consistently beats it on tabular data.
✕
SVM
No calibrated probabilities natively. Brier scoring requires honest probabilities.
✕
Naive Bayes
Assumes all features independent. EPA and ELO are clearly correlated.
✕
Analytical calc.
32 teams × 18 weeks × playoffs = billions of paths. Mathematically intractable.
